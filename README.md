# HarmonyHub 🎵 Adeline's Music & Motherhood Sanctuary

A dedicated web UI custom-crafted for **Adeline** — Singapore Primary School MOE Music Teacher and Mother of 2.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🎼 Singapore MOE Primary Music Syllabus (Primary 1 to 6)
- **Primary 1 (7yo)**: Beat vs Rhythm, Sol-Mi with Kodály hand signs, Animal soundscapes, Sing *Chan Mali Chan*, *Dayung Sampan*.
- **Primary 2 (8yo)**: Sol-Mi-La, Dynamics (*Forte/Piano*), Saint-Saëns *Carnival of the Animals*, Rhythmic Ostinato.
- **Primary 3 (9yo)**: Soprano Recorder introduction (B-A-G, gentle candle-breath technique), Malay Kompang (*Pak & Bung* strikes), 2-part rounds.
- **Primary 4 (10yo)**: Extended recorder range (low C-D-E), Angklung interlocking bamboo ensemble, Singapore National Day repertoire (*Home*, *Count on Me Singapore*).
- **Primary 5 (11yo)**: Ukulele chords (C, Am, F, G), Calypso strumming, Digital Music Loops (GarageBand / Soundtrap), Song structure analysis.
- **Primary 6 (12yo)**: Southeast Asian Heritage (Gamelan Slendro/Pelog scales, Kulintang), Capstone Ensemble, Graduation arrangements.

### 2. ✨ Daily Music Teaching Inspiration & Idea Generator
- 20+ ready-to-teach games & warmups (*The Beat Detective*, *Dalcroze Ribbon Dynamics*, *The Warm Candle Breath*, *Human Piano Keys*).
- 1-click **"Spin for Inspiration"** with pentatonic bell audio chime.
- Direct **"Save to Today's Plan"** or **"Draft Lesson Plan"** builder.

### 3. 🧸 Mom of 2 — Kids' Matters Hub
- **Two Customizable Children Profiles** (Names, grades, routines, special memos).
- **Daily School & Routine Checklist**: Homework / Ting Xie (Chinese spelling), English spelling, packing school bags, water bottle tracking, bedtime reading.
- **Musical Motherhood Sparks (Home Edition)**: 10-minute screen-free bonding games (*Living Room Opera Tidy-Up*, *Kitchen Pot-and-Pan Drum Circle*, *Bedtime Soundscape Storytelling*).
- **Medical & Teacher Notes Scratchpad** with automatic saving.

### 4. 📋 Unified Daily Task Logger & Routine Tracker
- **Smart Filters**: `All`, `🏫 MOE Music (School)`, `🏡 2 Kids & Home`, `✨ Me-Time`.
- **Time-Blocked Rhythm**: Morning rush, morning periods, afternoon CCA/prep, kid transit, and evening family wind-down.
- **Interactive Solfège Pitch Bells** (Do, Re, Mi, Fa, Sol, La, Ti, Do') powered by Web Audio API.
- **Daily Tempo Mood Tracker** (*Largo*, *Andante*, *Moderato*, *Allegro*, *Vivace*).
- **Mindful Gratitude & Win of the Day Log**.

### 5. 💾 Zero-Setup, Data Persistence & Privacy
- Runs directly in any web browser without needing servers or npm installs.
- All tasks, lesson plans, and notes are automatically saved to browser `localStorage`.
- Includes **Backup to JSON** and **Restore** features so her data is never lost.
- **Print-friendly styling** for clipping onto a music room clipboard.
