# HarmonyHub 🎵 Adeline's Music & Motherhood Sanctuary
### Mobile-First Edition (Optimized for Handphone, iPad & Tablet)

A dedicated, mobile-friendly web companion custom-crafted for **Adeline** — Singapore Primary School MOE Music Teacher and Mother of 2.

---

## 📱 Mobile & Tablet Optimizations

### 1. 🖐️ Thumb-Friendly Mobile Bottom Navigation
- **Today**: Fast access to her daily task logger, tempo mood, and Solfège bells.
- **Syllabus**: Complete P1-P6 Singapore MOE Music curriculum matrix.
- **Center (+) Action Button**: Instant mobile bottom drawer to quick-add a task, spin a 5-min music game, or draft a lesson plan.
- **Sparks**: 20+ ready-to-teach games, Dalcroze movement, Kodály drills, and Singapore heritage activities.
- **2 Kids**: Dedicated dual-track routines for both children.

### 2. 👶 Segmented Kid Switcher on Mobile Phones
- On phones, a clean segmented toggle (`[ 🧒 Ray (P3) ]` / `[ 👧 Chloe (P1) ]`) lets Adeline switch between her children with one tap without having to scroll through long pages.
- On tablets and iPad, both cards automatically display side-by-side!

### 3. 🔔 Pocket Solfège Bells
- Horizontal smooth-scrolling glockenspiel bells (`Do, Re, Mi, Fa, Sol, La, Ti, Do'`) powered by Web Audio synthesis for pitch checking right from her phone.

### 4. 📲 Use as a Native Mobile Phone App ("Add to Home Screen")
Adeline can save HarmonyHub to her phone screen so it opens in fullscreen just like an App Store app:
- **iPhone / iPad (Safari)**: Tap the **Share** button (`[↑]`) &rarr; tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
- **Android Phone / Tablet (Chrome)**: Tap the 3 dots (`⋮`) &rarr; tap **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install app"**.

### 5. 🔤 iOS Auto-Zoom Fix
- Mobile form fields have 16px font sizing on touch screens to prevent Apple Safari's irritating auto-zoom behavior on input focus.
- Touch targets are expanded to at least 44x44px for effortless tapping on the move.
